# 1. Assessment Philosophy

Assessment is a learning tool, not a punishment.

The purpose of assessment is to:

- Measure understanding.
- Identify weaknesses.
- Reinforce learning.
- Recommend future practice.

Learners should leave every assessment with a better understanding of their mistakes.

---

# 2. Assessment Types

## Lesson Assessment

Occurs after every lesson.

Purpose

Verify understanding of the concept taught during that lesson.

Examples

- Pronunciation check
- Listening exercise
- Identification
- Multiple choice
- Practical recitation

---

## Module Assessment

Occurs after completing an entire module.

Purpose

Verify the learner can combine multiple concepts together.

---

## Practice Assessment

Occurs during free practice sessions.

Purpose

Measure long-term retention rather than new learning.

---

## AI Pronunciation Assessment

Occurs whenever the learner records their recitation.

Purpose

Evaluate pronunciation quality and provide actionable feedback.

# 4. Evaluation Principles

Assessments should measure:

- Understanding
- Application
- Consistency
- Improvement

They should not reward memorization alone.

---

# 5. Feedback Principles

Every assessment should answer:

- What was correct?
- What was incorrect?
- Why was it incorrect?
- How can it be improved?

Feedback should remain constructive and encouraging.

---

# 6. Retry Philosophy

Learners should always have the opportunity to improve.

Retrying is encouraged.

Improvement is valued more than first-attempt perfection.

---

# 7. Completion vs Mastery

Completion

The learner has attempted the assessment.

Mastery

The learner consistently demonstrates correct understanding over repeated attempts and practice sessions.

Completion is immediate.

Mastery develops over time.

---

# 8. AI Evaluation

The AI may evaluate:

- Pronunciation
- Letter articulation
- Rule application
- Fluency
- Consistency

The AI should explain every detected mistake whenever possible.

---

# 9. Progress Tracking

Assessment results contribute to:

- Lesson Progress
- Module Progress
- Overall Learning Progress
- Future Practice Recommendations

Future versions may also include long-term pronunciation improvement tracking.

---

# 10. Assessment Outcomes

After every assessment the learner should know:

- What they performed well.
- What requires improvement.
- What to practice next.
- Whether they are ready to continue.

---

# 11. Future Expansion

The framework should support future assessment types including:

- Hifz evaluation
- Teacher-reviewed assessments
- Live recitation sessions
- Certification exams

without changing the core assessment philosophy.

---

# 12. Guiding Principle

Assessment should build confidence.

Every learner should finish an assessment feeling more informed and more capable than before they started.
