## 9. Completion Rules

A lesson is complete when:

- All learning sections are viewed.
- Practice is attempted.
- Assessment is completed.

Completion does not necessarily mean mastery.

---

## 10. Mastery Rules

A learner reaches mastery when:

- They consistently perform well in assessments.
- AI detects minimal recurring pronunciation mistakes.
- They demonstrate confidence across repeated practice.

Mastery may improve or decline over time based on continued practice.
